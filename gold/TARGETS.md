# Next gold-set targets (insurer / bancassurance)

RBI's PDF host blocks scripted downloads, and the 2025 consolidated directions below are PDF-only on their pages.
Download the PDFs manually in a browser (from the `Master Directions` page for each id) and ingest locally.

| id | Document | Insurance relevance |
|---|---|---|
| 13162 | Commercial Banks – Undertaking of Financial Services, 2025 | insurance agency/broking, departmental vs subsidiary |
| 13140 | Commercial Banks – Responsible Business Conduct, 2025 | mis-selling, third-party products |
| 13130 | Small Finance Banks – Undertaking of Financial Services | same family |
| 13058 / 13033 / 13007 | RRB / UCB / Rural Co-op – Undertaking of Financial Services | same family, differing limits |
| 12961 / 12984 | NBFC / AIFI – Undertaking of Financial Services | same family |
| 13139 | Commercial Banks – Managing Risks in Outsourcing | insurance back-office outsourcing |
| 13641 | Commercial Banks – Fraud Risk Management, 2026 | claims/premium fraud reporting |
| — | IRDAI (Insurance Brokers / Corporate Agents / Bancassurance) regulations | the insurer-side rules; irdai.gov.in/web/guest/home loads, adapter not built |

labelling one then diffing the others is much cheaper than starting from scratch.
